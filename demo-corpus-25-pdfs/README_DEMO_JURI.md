## 3. International Jury Live Demo Script (Feature-by-Feature)

### Demo Step 1: Instant Executive Dashboard & DuckDB Table Analytics (`Home` / `Analytics`)
- Log in with `waicy.demo@documind.org` -> show instant bootstrap of **25 Ready Documents**, hundreds of indexed pages, extracted benchmark tables, and verified quantitative metrics.
- Open **Analytics** and run a natural-language chart/SQL query across extracted benchmark tables.

### Demo Step 2: Multi-PDF Grounded Chat Citing 4+ Documents (`AI Chat`)
- Keep Scope on **All Repository Documents (25)** and paste:
  > *"Compare how RAGTruth, RAGAS, SafetyBench, and NIST AI RMF 1.0 evaluate and mitigate AI hallucinations and safety risks, with page citations from each document."*
- Click the inline `[1] [2] [3] [4]` badges to open the **Evidence Drawer** and jump directly to the exact cited page and bounding box across multiple PDFs!
- Open **Chat History** (`Clock` icon) and click the saved conversation to show persisted citations reloading instantaneously.

### Demo Step 3: Outlier Precision & Zero-Noise Test (`AI Chat` + `Knowledge Graph`)
- Open **Graph / Relationships** to show the 22 AI/Governance/Education/Climate/Biomedical papers forming dense thematic clusters while the **3 Outlier PDFs** (Caribbean Coral Reefs, Swedish Honeybees, Coffee Zirconia Ceramics) stay cleanly separated without false-positive hallucinations.
- Then return to **AI Chat** (`All Repository Documents`) and ask:
  > *"What happened to assisted sexual coral recruits during the 2023 Caribbean mass bleaching event, and how do native Swedish honeybee drones compare to Buckfast hybrids?"*
- DocuMind immediately pinpoints PDF #22 and PDF #23 out of 25 files with exact page citations.

### Demo Step 4: Cross-Document Synthesis Matrix (`Synthesis`)
- Select 4 PDFs (`RAGTruth`, `NIST AI RMF 1.0`, `Dr.Academy`, `Climate Policy Transformer`) and click **Run Synthesis** to generate a structured 4-dimension comparison matrix, consensus highlights, and page-cited takeaways.

### Demo Step 5: Interactive Comprehension Quiz & Storytelling Voice (`Studio`)
- Select `RAGTruth` or `Dr.Academy` in **Studio**:
  - **Quiz**: Choose `5Q` -> **Start Quiz** -> pick an option -> click **Check Answer** -> inspect page explanation -> **Finish & View Score**.
  - **Document Storytelling Voice**: Pick **Storytelling** style + **Marin** or **Coral** HD narrator -> click **Create Story** -> click **Play Story** for cohesive chapter-by-chapter audio narration.
